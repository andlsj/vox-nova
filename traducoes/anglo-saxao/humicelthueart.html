Hū micel Þū eart

Éalā Dryhten, mīn God, þonne ic mid egesfullre wundrunge
behealde ealle þās weorolda þe þīne handa ġeworht,
ic geseo þā steorran, ic gehȳre þæt bremmende þunor,
þīn geweald geond ealle þās heofonwealderne gecȳþed.

Þā singe mīn sāwol, mīn Hǣlend God, þē:
Hū micel Þū eart! Hū micel Þū eart!
Þā singe mīn sāwol, mīn Hǣlend God, þē:
Hū micel Þū eart! Hū micel Þū eart!

And þonne ic þence, þæt God, His Sunu ne āriende,
sende Hine to sweltenne, ic ne mæg hit eaþe onfōn,
þæt on þære Rōde, mīn byrþen glædlīċe berende,
Hē blēd and swelt to niman aweg mīne synne.

Þā singe mīn sāwol, mīn Hǣlend God, þē:
Hū micel Þū eart! Hū micel Þū eart!
Þā singe mīn sāwol, mīn Hǣlend God, þē:
Hū micel Þū eart! Hū micel Þū eart!

Þonne Crist cymþ, mid hlūd gewinne,
And me hām nimeþ, hwæt blisse gefylleþ min heorte.
Þonne ic būge, on ēaðmōdre ārwurde,
And þonne gecȳþe: "Mīn God, hū micel Þū eart!"

Þā singe mīn sāwol, mīn Hǣlend God, þē:
Hū micel Þū eart! Hū micel Þū eart!
Þā singe mīn sāwol, mīn Hǣlend God, þē:
Hū micel Þū eart! Hū micel Þū eart!

How Great Thou Art

O Lord my God, When I in awesome wonder,
Consider all the worlds Thy Hands have made;
I see the stars, I hear the rolling thunder,
Thy power throughout the universe displayed.

Then sings my soul, My Saviour God, to Thee,
How great Thou art, How great Thou art.
Then sings my soul, My Saviour God, to Thee,
How great Thou art, How great Thou art!

And when I think, that God, His Son not sparing;
Sent Him to die, I scarce can take it in;
That on the Cross, my burden gladly bearing,
He bled and died to take away my sin.

Then sings my soul, My Saviour God, to Thee,
How great Thou art, How great Thou art.
Then sings my soul, My Saviour God, to Thee,
How great Thou art, How great Thou art!

When Christ shall come, with shout of acclamation,
And take me home, what joy shall fill my heart.
Then I shall bow, in humble adoration,
And then proclaim: "My God, how great Thou art!"

Then sings my soul, My Saviour God, to Thee,
How great Thou art, How great Thou art.
Then sings my soul, My Saviour God, to Thee,
How great Thou art, How great Thou art!
